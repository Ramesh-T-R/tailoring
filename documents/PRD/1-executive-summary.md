# 1. Executive Summary
**Product Vision:** To bridge the gap between traditional craftsmanship and digital precision by providing tailors with a "Scientific Atelier" workspace. The platform enables technical accuracy in cloth cutting, reduces material waste through 3D pre-visualization, and fosters a global community of open-source craftsmanship.

**Target Audience:**
- **Professional Tailors:** Seeking high-precision tools and project versioning.
- **Apprentices:** Learning through digital simulation and "Master Consultant" logic.
- **Bespoke Designers:** Creating modular, reusable "Master Recipes."
